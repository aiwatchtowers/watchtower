# Watchtower

Watchtower is a personal productivity tool that runs locally on your own
computer. It brings your workplace communications and calendar into one place
and uses AI to keep you on top of what matters.

## What it does

- **Slack & Jira sync** — pulls your messages and issues into a local database.
- **Google Calendar** — read-only sync of your events for meeting preparation.
- **AI digests & briefings** — daily summaries, decisions, and people insights.
- **Meeting prep** — talking points and open items before each meeting.

## Privacy

Watchtower runs entirely on your machine. There is no Watchtower server. Your
data stays in a local database that you control. See the
[Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.html).

## Google Calendar access

When you connect Google Calendar, Watchtower requests **read-only** access to
your events and calendar list, solely to generate meeting preparation and
briefings on your own device. It never modifies, deletes, or shares your
calendar.
