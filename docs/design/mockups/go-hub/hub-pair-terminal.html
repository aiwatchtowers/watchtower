3. SAS prompt

A phone scanned the code. Its name is the phone's own and passes sanitizeForTerminal. y or yes in any case confirms; anything else, an empty line, EOF or the code's expiry sends no.

Link your iPhone to raspberrypi

Confirm code 4827 on your phone? [y/N]
