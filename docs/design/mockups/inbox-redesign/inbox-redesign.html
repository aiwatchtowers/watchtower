What changes and why

One brief, not twenty

Now: a "Morning brief" row for every unread day — 17 identical rows.

One Morning brief row, always today's. Past days live inside it: ‹ › stepper and a History menu. Past briefs leave the list.

Counts that mean "act"

Now: 747, 200 waiting, 1689 unread, 546 to judge — nobody can act on these.

Each row counts only what is new or what you can finish today: 12 people waiting, 6 threads with news, 13 ideas for today. The backlog is a quiet second line. The sidebar badge = Needs approval + new waiting.

The row tells you what is inside

Now: a grey dot, a title and "updated 3 h ago".

A coloured tile per kind, the first real line of content (who asked what, what moved), time on the right, count pill. Unread is a blue dot next to the title.

Never "Select a card"

Now: an empty right half with a stray line through it.

The pane opens today's brief on arrival and remembers the last selection. Every pane is one centred reading column (760 pt).

Sections by urgency

Now: one flat list, sorted by time.

Needs you → Today → Running. Needs approval stays in the rail with an orange count because it blocks agents.

A narrow folder rail

Now: a 480 pt column holding two rows.

196 pt rail, collapsible (⌥⌘S). Folders only appear once you have one. New Artifact… and New Folder sit at the bottom as one row.

Waiting for me is a triage queue

Now: 200 rows of the same weight.

Today / This week / Older. Older than 7 days folds with one "Mark All Done" button. Keyboard: J/K, E done, R reply, S snooze.

Backlogs come in batches

Now: "546 to judge" — a wall.

Idea finder hands out a daily batch (13) as one card at a time: Keep / Drop / Later. Track my threads shows only threads with news; 240 quiet ones fold.
