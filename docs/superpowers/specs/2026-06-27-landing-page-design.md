**Type:** Functional content spec (sections + messaging, not visual design)
**Goal:** Product homepage that converts visitors to **download / try** the app.

## Positioning

- **Audience:** anyone drowning in Slack (broad, not role-specific).
- **Hero promise:** *"Your AI chief of staff for Slack"* — proactive, not another bot you have to query.
- **Primary CTA:** Download for macOS.
- **Core differentiator threaded throughout:** privacy-first (runs locally) + proactive (brings things to you).

### Cross-cutting emotional triggers (push everywhere)
*Control* over chaos · *Peace of mind* (nothing slips) · *Proactivity* (AI works for you) · *Privacy* (your data stays yours) · *Time saved* · *One connected picture* (no more tool-hopping).

## Section-by-section

### 1. Hero
- **H1:** "Your AI chief of staff for Slack"
- **Subhead:** AI reads all your channels locally and hands you your day every morning — what got decided, who's waiting on you, what's on fire.
- **Primary CTA:** Download for macOS
- **Secondary CTA:** See how it works (scroll / demo)
- **Privacy microcopy** under the button: "Runs locally. Your data never leaves your machine."
- **Visual proof:** screenshot of the daily briefing (placeholder).

### 2. Problem — what we press on
Three pains, emotional angle "I'm losing control":
- You're drowning in hundreds of messages and threads — impossible to read it all.
- Slack shows you the @-mention, but not what it was about, whether it still needs you, or what to do about it. Decisions get made in threads and recorded nowhere — a week later no one remembers where it landed.
- Your day starts with chaos instead of a plan.

**Plus the deeper pain — your tools are siloed:** Jira is disconnected from the work actually happening, Slack talks to nothing, your calendar lives in another tab. Everything is in a different place and nothing is connected.

### 3. How it works — 3 steps (+ privacy)
1. Connect your Slack workspace.
2. AI reads and analyzes it **locally** — on your machine.
3. Get your briefing, inbox and tasks — every day.

Reinforce here: nothing is sent to a third-party server.

### 4. Core capabilities — features as benefits
Cards, each framed pain → benefit (not a tech feature):
- **Daily Briefing** — your whole day on one screen.
- **Catch-up & Digests** — missed a day? Catch up in minutes, not an hour.
- **Inbox** — no @-mention or DM left hanging.
- **Tracks** — threads of decisions and discussions assemble themselves.
- **People** — see who's working on what across the team. *(visibility framing — who owns/drives what, NOT surveillance or per-person risk scoring)*
- **Targets** — your tasks pulled from conversations, not from memory.
- **Calendar & Meeting Prep** — walk into every meeting prepared.

### 5. Why it's different — Trust (rational push)
- **Connects your stack, no migration:** your tools are old and siloed — Jira detached from the real work, Slack wired to nothing, calendar in its own tab. Watchtower stitches them into one connected experience and a new way of working — **without making you switch tools**. Keep Slack, Jira, Calendar; we make them finally talk.
- **Privacy-first:** local SQLite, your data never leaves your machine.
- **Your LLM, your choice:** use your existing claude/codex CLI subscription, bring your own API keys, or run a fully local LLM. No vendor lock-in.
- **Native macOS** — not another browser tab.
- **Proactive, not reactive:** it brings things to you instead of waiting to be asked.
