# Live Link Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A tap on the phone shows at once and is confirmed by the tower in ~150–300 ms; waking the app brings the replica up to date in under a second.

**Architecture:** The relay delivers every record of a save inside its `ZoneChanged` event (one write, stored and delivered in the same hop). Hub and phone send ops over their open socket and apply inline records without a read. The hub handles mailbox events on their own lane; the phone applies the expected effect of an action as a pending overlay and wakes with a 300 ms ping, a parallel redial and pipelined reads.

**Tech Stack:** relay — Cloudflare Worker (TypeScript, vitest), Go client, protobuf (buf); hub — Go (`internal/hub`); phone — Swift (WatchtowerKit, WatchtowerMobile, GRDB).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-live-link-design.md` (approved, ask #1535); mockups `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-live-link-mockups.html`.

## Global Constraints

- Frame budget for inline records: 256 KiB per `ZoneChanged`; a single record over it goes without its body.
- An event goes out only after the write is durable; the old single `record` field stays filled for a one-record save (old clients).
- Capability string advertised in `HelloOk.capabilities`: `"records_inline"`. Any side without it behaves exactly as today.
- Wake ping deadline 300 ms (named constant); overlay rules: 30 s after an `applied` echo without a visible effect, 10 min without an echo.
- Overlay captions are the existing `BoardWriteText` strings only: "Sending…", "Waiting for your tower", "Your tower could not make this change". No new strings, colors only through the phone's tone tokens.
- No screen changes beyond the overlays shown in the mockups; latency numbers go to the latency tables and the log, not to Settings.
- Public repo: no live-install ids, numbers or paths in code, tests or docs.
- Tests never wait on wall-clock (fake clocks / synctest); a test that starts a process reaps its group.
- Branches: relay tasks → a branch in `~/IdeaProjects/relay`, PR into its `main`; Watchtower tasks → task PRs into the umbrella branch `feature/live-link` (draft umbrella PR into `main` until the last task).
- Inner loop only per task (`go test ./internal/hub/<pkg>`, `make kit-test FILTER=…`, `make mobile-test MOBILE_FILTER=…`, `make lint-diff`, `make lint-swift`); the full gate once per phase by the controller.

## Review Focus

1. A socket that dies after an op was sent over it: the op may have run. The fallback to HTTPS must re-send only conditional (seq-checked) saves and treat "conflict with its own record" as success — test in T4 and T9.
2. The relay's fan-out merges queued changes for a slow reader: a merge must keep the records only when they are contiguous and within the budget, else carry none (the reader then reads) — test in T1.
3. The relay drops a record for a phone over its traffic budget (`trafficOver`): the phone must fall back to a read, never skip — test in T8.
4. The app killed with overlays pending: overlays live in memory (they last seconds, at most 10 min); on relaunch the pending outbox rows show as today with their caption and nothing is lost — test in T11.
5. A phone with two towers: overlays and inline cursors are per tower; an echo of tower A never clears an overlay of tower B — test in T11.
